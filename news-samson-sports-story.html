The Inside Story of Samson Sports

From barangay courts to a growing sports brand, the story of Michael Glenn Samson is built around basketball, affordability, trust and community.

Featured by Rebanse • October 7, 2026 • Original reporting by Martin Dale D. Bolima

Samson Sports grew from a lifelong relationship with basketball. Michael Glenn Samson started around eight years old, watching games with family and experiencing the excitement of local basketball in Pampanga. More than three decades later, basketball remains part of his life as a player, coach, team leader and sports entrepreneur.

His path was not always a straight line. After school, Michael spent years focused on corporate work and supporting his family. His connection to basketball returned through coaching and community teams, eventually leading to deeper involvement in Pampanga leagues and the creation and development of teams including the Pampanga Raptors.

From Player to Coach to Entrepreneur

Coaching changed the way Michael saw the game. Instead of focusing only on his own role, he learned to look at the entire team, prepare players and make decisions under pressure. That experience later became an important part of how he approaches customers and the Samson Sports brand.

Samson Sports began in 2024 as an online sports business connected closely to the basketball community. Through his involvement in local leagues, Michael was able to understand what players were actually looking for in shoes and sports gear. The goal was not simply to sell products, but to make useful equipment more accessible to players who have to manage limited budgets.

“Quality, affordability, trust and genuine support for athletes” are central ideas behind what Michael wants Samson Sports to represent.

A Brand Built Around the Community

Because Michael is still active around basketball courts and leagues, Samson Sports developed through relationships, recommendations and community connections. The business reflects his belief that a sports seller can also be a helpful presence in the community—someone who understands the needs of players because he has experienced them himself.

That philosophy also connects his coaching and business work. A coach supports players by understanding their needs, building trust and giving the right guidance. Michael applies the same mindset when helping athletes choose equipment that fits their needs and budget.

Looking Ahead

Samson Sports continues to operate primarily online while Michael balances his regular office work with coaching, playing and managing the business. His longer-term vision is to grow the brand beyond Pampanga and eventually establish a physical space where athletes can find affordable gear while also feeling connected to the basketball community.

For Michael, Samson Sports is therefore more than a retail name. It represents a basketball journey that moved from playing, to coaching, to providing and giving back.
