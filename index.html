The Person Behind the Brand

Michael Glenn Samson

Michael Glenn Samson is a basketball player, coach and the founder of Samson Sports. His basketball journey began around age eight and developed through playing, coaching, team leadership and community involvement.

Coaching became a focused part of the journey around 2019, with Best Coach recognition in PPL and DHBL leagues.
