Raptors Story

From Marcons Jokers to Raptors.

This is the real journey of the team — from its beginnings at Marcons PH1 in 2020, through lockdown, the birth of the Pampanga Basketball League, the creation of the Raptors identity, multiple leagues and Finals appearances, and finally a championship.

2020
THE BEGINNING

Marcons Jokers

The story started at Marcons PH1, bringing together players from La Rose Noire and Marcons PH1. The team was known as Marcons Jokers, wearing a purple and black alternate jersey.

The team eventually joined the newly built Cabalen Basketball League (CBA) in Angeles City. The campaign was then stopped by the COVID-19 lockdown.

2022
A NEW CHAPTER

Pampanga Basketball League

In 2022, Michael Glenn Samson and his friend Mark Manalus, CEO of PPL, started the Pampanga Basketball League. Marcons Jokers returned to competition in a maroon jersey.

This became the point where the team began a journey of continuously joining different leagues and competitions.

2022–23
BUILDING THE TEAM

Maroon, Beige & Dark Grey

Marcons Jokers continued in PPL and finished 2nd Runner-Up. The team later competed as The Boys in beige, using the same lineup and jersey in Hafa Adai.

A dark grey jersey was added. PPL and Hafa Adai campaigns ended in the Quarterfinals, followed by another PPL season with the beige and dark grey uniforms, again ending in the Quarterfinals.

RAPTORS
THE NAME IS BORN

The First Raptors Uniform

While preparing for Hafa Adai, the team originally planned a purple colorway. That idea inspired the name Raptors, based on the Toronto Raptors and their NBA-style uniform concept.

But the purple jersey was never printed. Instead, the team received a maroon and golden-yellow alternate jersey. That unexpected uniform became the first Raptors jersey — and the new identity was born.

The Raptors competed in both Hafa Adai and PPL, with both campaigns ending in the Quarterfinals.

NEXT ERA
YELLOW RAPTORS

The Raptors Start Making Their Mark

The yellow Raptors jersey arrived in the next PPL season. In an open-age, open-lineup league with no restrictions, the team finished 1st Runner-Up.

THE RUN
FINALS & PODIUMS

More Leagues. More Battles.

The Raptors continued into Astro Basketball League Season 2, again reaching the Semifinals. In Quarenta Ballers 35+, the team finished 1st Runner-Up, losing to JAV Ballers in the Finals.

In PPL Exclusive, wearing a Gilas Pilipinas blue-style jersey, the Raptors again finished 1st Runner-Up, once again losing to JAV Ballers in the Finals.

AUTOKRANK
AUTOKRANK RAPTORS

A Fresh Identity

The team introduced the Autocrank Raptors jerseys and continued across Hafa Adai, Clark Papawis, Quarenta Ballers, Kumpares and PPL Angeles.

Hafa Adai — Semifinals / 2nd Runner-UpClark Papawis — 3rd Runner-Up🥈 Quarenta Ballers Open Age Season 2 — 1st Runner-UpKumpares Open Age — QuarterfinalsPPL Angeles — Quarterfinals
NEXT GENERATION
WHITE & DARK BLUE

Another Raptors Chapter

A new white and dark blue Raptors jersey was introduced for an open-lineup PPL season featuring several interbarangay players. The team finished 3rd Runner-Up.

In DHBL Exclusive, wearing white and red, the Raptors reached the Quarterfinals. Then came the season that changed the story.

🏆
DHBL EXCLUSIVE · SEASON 2

THE CHAMPIONSHIP

Wearing a purple and gold Lakers-style jersey, the Raptors finally captured a championship.

FIRST CHAMPIONSHIP

After years of Quarterfinal exits, Semifinal runs, Finals appearances and podium finishes, the Raptors reached the top.

TODAY
HUSTLE BALLERS × RAPTORS

The Story Continues

Today, the team continues under the name Hustle Ballers × Raptors, competing in three leagues.

CBL — SemifinalsCFA — SemifinalsSunday Papawis League — Semifinals

The jerseys changed. The lineups changed. The leagues changed. But the Raptors kept going.

THE RAPTORS LEGACY

One team. Many chapters. Still writing the story.

From Marcons Jokers to Raptors, the journey has never been about one jersey, one roster, or one league. It is a story of players coming together, competing, learning, returning and building something that lasts.
