🏀 THE RAPTORS STORY

2020 — Where It All Began: Marcons Jokers

The story started in 2020 at Marcons PH1, with a group of players from La Rose Noire and Marcons PH1 coming together to play basketball. At that time, the team was known as Marcons Jokers, wearing a purple and black alternate jersey. The team eventually joined the newly established Cabalen Basketball League (CBA) in Angeles City. However, the basketball journey was temporarily put on hold when the COVID-19 lockdown brought local basketball activities to a stop. But the team was not finished.

2022 — The Beginning of a New Chapter

In 2022, Michael Glenn Samson and his friend Mark Manalus, CEO of PPL, started the Pampanga Basketball League (PPL). The team returned to competition under the name Marcons Jokers, this time wearing a maroon jersey. This became an important turning point, and the journey never stopped.

2022–2023 — Building the Team

The Marcons Jokers continued into another PPL season, finishing 2nd Runner-Up. The team later introduced a new identity known as The Boys, wearing a beige jersey, and later added a dark grey jersey, campaigning through the Hafa Adai Basketball League and PPL.

🦖 THE BIRTH OF THE RAPTORS

Inspired by the Toronto Raptors, the team decided to call itself the Raptors. Though a planned purple uniform was never printed, the team ended up with a maroon and golden-yellow alternate jersey. And that became the first Raptors uniform. The Raptors were born.

🏆 THE RAPTORS START MAKING THEIR MARK

The next PPL season introduced the yellow Raptors jersey, where the team finished 🥈 1st Runner-Up in an open-age league. They expanded into Astro Basketball League (Semifinals), Quarenta Ballers (🥈 1st Runner-Up), and 3J League 35+ (Quarterfinals).

🔥 MORE LEAGUES. MORE FINALS.

The Raptors returned to Astro League Season 2 (Semifinals), Quarenta Ballers 35+ (🥈 1st Runner-Up), and PPL Exclusive wearing a Gilas Pilipinas-inspired blue jersey (🥈 1st Runner-Up).

🔵 AUTOKRANK RAPTORS ERA

Introducing a fresh identity as the Autocrank Raptors, the team competed across Hafa Adai (2nd Runner-Up), Clark Papawis League (3rd Runner-Up), Quarenta Ballers Open Age Season 2 (🥈 1st Runner-Up), Kumpares, and PPL Angeles.

⚪🔵 A NEW GENERATION & 🏆 THE CHAMPIONSHIP

Following a 3rd Runner-Up finish with their white and dark blue inter-barangay lineup, the Raptors entered DHBL Exclusive Season 2 with a completely different look: Purple and Gold — Lakers-style jersey. After years of hard work, the team finally captured their 🏆 FIRST CHAMPIONSHIP.

🔥 TODAY — HUSTLE BALLERS × RAPTORS

Today, the team carries the name Hustle Ballers × Raptors, competing across CBL, CFA, and Sunday Papawis League (all reaching Semifinals). The names, jerseys, and players may evolve, but the purpose remains: keep playing, keep competing, keep building.

“From Marcons Jokers to Raptors, the journey has never been about one jersey, one roster, or one league. The jerseys changed. The lineups changed. The leagues changed. But the Raptors kept going. And the story is still being written.”
