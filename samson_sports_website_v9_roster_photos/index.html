About

Michael Glenn Samson

Michael Glenn Samson is a basketball player and coach and the founder of Samson Sports. His basketball journey began around age eight and has developed through playing, coaching, team leadership and community involvement.

Coaching became a focused part of his journey around 2019, with documented Best Coach recognition in PPL and DHBL leagues.

“A team, a community, a legacy.”
The Brand

Samson Sports

Established in 2024, Samson Sports is a sports-focused online business selling basketball shoes and sports gear through social media, with TikTok serving as the checkout point.

The website brings the brand's basketball community, Raptors activities, media and retail presence together in one place.
